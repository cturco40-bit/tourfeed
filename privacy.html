Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data including pages visited, time on site, and device type. We do not collect personal information beyond what you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely to send you TourFeed newsletters and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time. Analytics data is used to understand how visitors use our site and improve the experience.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics for website analytics, Beehiiv for email newsletters, and Supabase for data storage. Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies via Google Analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Data Retention

We retain your email address until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention policies.
